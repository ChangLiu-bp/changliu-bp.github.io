**Location:** Budapest, Hungary

## About the Chapter

The **IEEE Hungary Section Intelligent Transportation Systems Society (ITSS) Chapter** was officially established on 14 December 2025.

The Chapter aims to promote research, education, and technological innovation in Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), while strengthening collaboration among academia, industry, and public-sector organizations in Hungary.

As part of the global IEEE ITSS community, the Chapter provides opportunities for technical exchange, professional networking, international collaboration, and student engagement.

Our activities include:

- Organizing technical seminars, workshops, and research events.
- Connecting researchers, industry professionals, and public-sector stakeholders.
- Supporting students and young professionals in ITS research and innovation.
- Promoting international collaboration with other IEEE ITSS Chapters.
- Encouraging interdisciplinary research in connected and automated mobility.

## Chapter Events and Activities

### November 2026 — 5th IEEE ITSS Region 8 Chapters Meeting

**Date:** 5–6 November 2026  
**Location:** Queen Mary University of London (QMUL), London, UK

The IEEE ITSS Hungary Chapter participated in the 5th IEEE ITSS Region 8 Chapters Meeting in London.

Representing the Hungary Chapter, Chair Dr. Chang Liu and colleague Gergely Kovács joined representatives from other European IEEE ITSS Chapters to exchange experiences, share chapter activities, and explore opportunities for future collaboration.

The meeting provided an opportunity to strengthen international connections and discuss initiatives supporting the development of the ITS community across IEEE Region 8.

### November 2026 — IEEE ITSS Hungary Chapter Local Seminar

**Date:** 12 November 2026  
**Time:** 16:00–18:15 CET  
**Location:** Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME), I Building, Room IB023

**Theme:**

*Simulations, Pilots and Deployments: Experiences from the Hungarian C-ITS Ecosystem*

**Organized by:** IEEE ITSS Hungary Chapter

**Co-organizers:** BME-HIT and HUN-REN SZTAKI

**General Chair:** Dr. Chang Liu  
**Programme Chair:** Dr. László Bokor

The seminar brings together researchers, industry professionals, public-sector representatives, and students to exchange knowledge and practical experiences in Cooperative Intelligent Transportation Systems (C-ITS).

The programme features seven invited speakers from academia, industry, and the public sector, covering intelligent traffic management, multimodal journey planning, C-ITS technologies, and real-world deployments.

### December 2025 — Establishment of the IEEE ITSS Hungary Chapter

**Date:** 14 December 2025

The IEEE Hungary Section Intelligent Transportation Systems Society Chapter was officially established.

The Chapter was founded to strengthen the local ITS research and professional community and to foster closer collaboration with the international IEEE ITSS network.

Dr. Chang Liu serves as the founding Chapter Chair, together with Vice Chair Dr. László Bokor and Secretary Mengke Dalai.
