I supervise BSc and MSc thesis projects at the **Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME)**, focusing on artificial intelligence, computer vision, autonomous systems, intelligent transportation, and remote sensing.

My students work on both theoretical and applied research, including multimodal perception, UAV-based systems, satellite imagery analysis, and autonomous navigation.
